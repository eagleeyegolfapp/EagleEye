Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. EagleEye does not require an EagleEye cloud account. Use Settings (gear) → Data in the App to export a full backup before switching phones or major changes.

I finished rounds but some features are locked

EagleEye includes a free trial of 3 finished rounds or 60 completed holes (whichever comes first). Deleted finished rounds still count toward the trial. After the trial, advanced features (for example deep stats, flyover, goals) require a one-time unlock via Apple In-App Purchase. Scoring a round still works while locked; your data remains on the device. Use Restore Purchases on the unlock screen if you already bought on this Apple ID.

Purchases and refunds

Payments are handled by Apple. For billing or refunds, use your Apple ID purchase history / Apple Support. We cannot refund charges directly outside Apple’s process.

Do I need an OpenStreetMap account?

No. Flyover maps associate from the course library using public OpenStreetMap data. You do not create or pair an OSM account inside EagleEye.

Location, camera, or photos prompts

  • Location — optional (weather / flyover GPS). Not required to score.
  • Camera / Photos — optional (scorecard scan or saving images you request).

Change access anytime in iOS Settings → EagleEye.

Demo Mode

Settings can load sample data so the App isn’t empty. Turning Demo Mode off removes sample (demo-tagged) content and keeps your real rounds.

Lost data

If something looks missing:

  • Confirm Demo Mode is not hiding your real rounds (Demo Mode shows sample data only).
  • Check Data & Backup for exports you made earlier.
  • Email support with what happened (update, reinstall, new phone) and we will try to help.

Feature requests & bugs

Email is best. Include steps to reproduce, screenshots if useful, and whether the issue happens in Demo Mode or with real rounds.
